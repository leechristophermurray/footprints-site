The Companion

Your study, your way.

Nine quiet tools, woven into one practice. None requires anything but your attention.

01 Read

KJV Bible study

Track your journey through every book and chapter of the King James Version, with personal notes and highlights along the way.

Persistent reading position
02 Move

Mindful walks

Pair scripture with movement. Take guided walks with spoken scripture, GPS tracking, and Health Connect integration for holistic wellness.

Health Connect
03 Reflect

Sacred reflection

A private journal space for your spiritual reflections. On-device AI offers gentle biblical context drawn from your own entries.

FLAG_SECURE journal
04 Insight

AI-powered insight

Fact-checking, related topics, and study suggestions powered by on-device AI — your data never leaves your phone.

Gemini Nano · on-device
05 Practice

Quizzes

Test your knowledge with quizzes generated from your study progress. Multiple modes keep things fresh and challenging.

Adaptive difficulty
06 Sync

Cross-device sync

Study on your phone, tablet, or Chromebook. End-to-end encrypted sync through your own Google Drive — no servers, no middlemen.

Zero-knowledge · AES-256-GCM
07 Listen

Listen to scripture

Two ways to hear the Word: verse-by-verse with your device's voice, or chapter-by-chapter as natural narration streamed from the cloud. Cached for offline once you've heard it.

TTS & streamed narration
08 Surfaces

Wear, watch, drive

A daily-verse complication and tile on Wear OS. A reader on Android TV with Cast from your phone. Voice-first scripture playback in Android Auto and AAOS-equipped vehicles.

Wear · TV · Auto · AAOS

Footprints Digital

A clean digital watch face for Wear OS — large gold clock, date row, four cardinal complications. Distributed as a separate listing on Google Play.

Standalone listing
The Conviction

Built on principles.

Footprints is designed with conviction: your spiritual life is deeply personal, and your data should stay that way. Every feature is built privacy-first, with on-device processing and zero-knowledge encryption.

There is no premium tier. Every user gets the full Footprints experience. The app is sustained by optional weekly contributions or a single rewarded ad — your choice.

Privacy-first Zero-knowledge encryption On-device AI No premium tier No data harvesting Public-domain scripture
Our quiet promise
  • i. Your data stays on your device. AI inference runs locally — never sent to a server.
  • ii. Sync is end-to-end encrypted. AES-256-GCM with Argon2id. Not even we can read it.
  • iii. Scripture is public domain. The KJV text and narrated audio belong to no one.
  • iv. Sustained by you, not by ads. $1 contribution or a single rewarded ad — your call, weekly.
